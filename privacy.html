RTO DocPrep privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

RTO DocPrep fills transport forms on your computer.

Everything you type into it (names, addresses, phone numbers, vehicle details) stays on your computer. The app has no account, sends nothing over the internet, and contains no tracking or advertising. No one, including us, receives what you type.

The PDFs, case files and backups you save go only where you choose. The app keeps your cases, your address book and its settings in its own folder on your computer; uninstalling the app deletes them.
